Relevant Projects

Cattle Monitoring and Management System

Startup project from the residency of the Digital Future Scholarship program. I developed a backend API using Python/FastAPI and PostgreSQL. The system provides ranchers with a useful platform to easily view and manage their cattle at any time, with analytical information and weight variation alerts, farm-based viewing, and weigh-ins automatically processed from cattle dimensions captured by sensors/cameras.

Gym Management System

Integrative project for the Digital Future Scholarship program. I developed a backend API using JavaScript/NestJS and MySQL. The system provides a complete gym platform, with role-based access control (Admin/Reception, Trainer, and Student), workout plans, progress monitoring, and student points-based gamification.

It can search for content across the internet, including images, videos, and Wikipedia pages; integrate with LLMs through @Groq to generate responses and interact with users; generate images through @pollinations.ai; and send alerts about free games on Steam and the Epic Games Store.
